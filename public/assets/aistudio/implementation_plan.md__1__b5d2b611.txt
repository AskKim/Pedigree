## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Combines accurate multi-species coat color genetics with transparent lineage-wide carrier tracking. Breeders can record **Confirmed**, **Suspected**, and **Cleared (Non-Carrier)** genetic traits alongside conservative Mendelian genotypes and polygenic modifiers like Rufus—propagating ancestry risks accurately without overwriting reality with optimistic breed-standard guesses.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Pedigree livestock and show breeders (starting with New Zealand Red rabbit breeders, scaling cleanly across all 11 predefined species plus custom species) who rely on truthful Punnett projections, clean show/sale pedigrees, and fast herd screening.
- **Key Value**: Prevents hidden surprises (e.g., two show Reds carrying `W` unexpectedly producing non-wideband offspring, or silent health-gene carriers in a bloodline) while honoring proven outcross test-breedings that clear a branch—and keeping the mandatory Genetics Verification Notice visible everywhere coat predictions appear until expert sign-off.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows

1. **Adding or Editing an Animal (Conservative Preset + Verification Gate)**
   - Creating a new animal starts with blank/unknown modifier slots rather than hardcoding `ww`.
   - Selecting a variety preset (e.g., *New Zealand Red*) fills only guaranteed visible loci (`A_ B_ C_ D_ ee __`) and opens an inline verification prompt:
     - Optional checkboxes such as *"Confirm Wide-Band (`ww` verified by pedigree or test breeding)"*—leaving it unchecked keeps `__` (`W_`/`__`), preventing false 100% wide-band locks.
   - Right alongside coat color fields sits the standalone **Rufus Intensity** control (`Unspecified`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`) for polygenic shade depth.
   - Below genotype loci, the **Hidden Genetic Traits & Carrier Disclosures** panel presents three synchronized lists: **Confirmed Carrier Of**, **Suspected Carrier Of**, and **Cleared / Confirmed Non-Carrier Of** (with quick proof selector: *Test Bred + breeding count*, *DNA Tested*, *Verified Homozygous Coat Locus*, or *Breeder Verified*).

2. **Viewing Profiles & Pedigrees (Sire-Side & Dam-Side Branch Firewall)**
   - On any animal profile and in 3–6 generation pedigree cells (in-app, Evans `.HTM`, and Printable Barn Binders), the engine walks upward through both parental branches independently:
     - If an ancestor is **Confirmed** or **Suspected** and no descendant between that ancestor and the subject is marked **Cleared** for that trait, the subject automatically displays quiet unboxed `"Lineage Potential: <Trait> (via <Parent>)"` context—distinct from directly entered Suspected/Confirmed traits.
     - If an animal in the middle of the Sire line is **Cleared** (e.g., *Dilute · Test Bred 4x* or verified `DD`), the Sire branch stops passing Dilute forward—while an uncleared Dam branch would still independently warn of potential Dam-side carriage.

3. **Screening the Herd (Carrier Finder Toolbar)**
   - In the Herd registry, a lightweight **Carrier Filter** control (filter by **Any Carrier / Potential**, **Confirmed Only**, **Suspected Only**, **Cleared Only**, or search a specific trait such as *Wide Band*, *Dilute*, or *Megacolon*) immediately surfaces matching animals with live counts without crowding card titles.

4. **Breeding Lab (Combined Genetic & Carrier Risk Panel)**
   - Selecting a Sire and Dam displays:
     - Split **Confirmed Punnett Outcomes** vs **Unconfirmed Carrier Possibilities** when either parent contains `_` or `__` (never generating fake `100% __` offspring or falsely guaranteeing `100% ww`).
     - Sex-split Male/Female Punnett rows for X-linked loci (Cat `O/o`).
     - Prominent **Breeding Risk Alerts** combining coat/lethal warnings (`EnEn` Charlie megacolon, `MM` Double Merle, `RnRn`, `WhWh`/`TOTO`, Ferret Blaze/Panda) and any shared **Confirmed / Suspected / Lineage-Potential** carrier traits between Sire and Dam.

5. **Spreadsheet / HerdBoss Imports**
   - Imported records populate variety and color labels cleanly without guessing unrecorded genotypes (e.g., `"Red"` never backfills `ww`), keeping imported animals safe until a breeder inspects and confirms them.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1 — Conservative Variety Presets + Interactive Verification Prompt**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Variety presets populate only phenotype-proven alleles (e.g., Red = `A_ B_ C_ D_ ee __`), leave unverified modifiers unknown (`__` / `allele_`), and prompt the breeder with optional verification toggles before committing optional recessive alleles like `ww`. Newly added Rabbits start blank until a variety or manual genotype is chosen.
  - *Why*: Eliminates the "New Zealand Red trap" where crossing two unverified Red rabbits falsely predicts 100% `ww` offspring, while keeping quick data entry fast for breeders who have genuinely test-bred their stock.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Leaving all genotypes permanently manual (too slow for casual entry) or auto-filling `ww` automatically (scientifically inaccurate for show herds).

- **Decision 2 — Polygenic Rufus Intensity Stored Independently of `W`/`w`**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Add a dedicated `rufusLevel` field (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`, or blank) with a clean profile selector, excluded from Mendelian Punnett allele crossing.
  - *Why*: Breed depth in Red New Zealands depends heavily on Rufus modifiers as well as Wide-Band (`ww`); conflating visual brightness with `ww` misleads both test-breeding inference and pedigree expectations.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Packing Rufus notes into free-text notes or carrier tags (harder to filter cleanly alongside color data).

- **Decision 3 — Branch-Level `Cleared` Firewall Evaluated Per Parental Line**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Add `clearedCarrierOf` storing trait name + structured clearance proof (`Test Bred` with count, `DNA Tested`, `Verified Homozygous Dominant Coat Locus`, or `Breeder Verified`), reconcilable with Confirmed/Suspected lists. Ancestral lineage propagation evaluates Sire and Dam paths separately to depth limit, halting upward traversal along any branch that hits a Cleared record—or a user-verified homozygous dominant coat locus such as `DD` or `WW` (strictly rejecting auto-filled/guessed genotypes).
  - *Why*: Mirrors real-world breeding math: proving a buck is `DD` or test-breeding him cleanly removes recessive risk through *his* half of the pedigree without hiding an independent risk coming down the doe's side.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Manual-only lineage clearing with no genotype assist (forces duplicate entry when `DD` is already DNA/test-proven) or global non-branch clearing (could wrongly mask the other parent's lineage risk).

- **Decision 4 — Strict Non-Assumption Boundary for Imports & Legacy Records**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Importers only record genotype strings explicitly present in genotype/DNA columns, and existing records loaded from SQLite preserve whatever `genotypeRaw` was stored without upgrading `"Red"` to `ww`.
  - *Why*: Prevents bulk CSV/HerdBoss imports from silently injecting unverified homozygous alleles into foundation pedigrees.

- **Decision 5 — Unified Species-Scoped Color Engine + Preserved Verification Notice**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Refactor the genetics core into a pure TypeScript `SpeciesGeneticsSpec` registry spanning all 11 predefined species (fixing homozygous-dominant inference, `allele_` slot normalization, intermediate recessive detection, `_` Punnett separation, Cat X-linked `O/o`, missing Rabbit varieties, lethal pairing alerts, and custom-species plain-text isolation) while keeping the expert-verification notice visible across every genetics surface.
  - *Why*: Prevents Rabbit dominance rules from mislabeling Cats, Dogs, Goats, Guinea Pigs, or custom species, and ensures domain-expert review precedes removing caution notices.

### Data Model & State Entities

- **Animal Record Extensions**:
  - `rufusLevel`: Optional enumerated modifier (`'Low' | 'Medium' | 'High' | ''`) for Red coat depth tracking independent of Mendelian loci.
  - `suspectedCarrierOf`: Normalized array of unconfirmed trait names.
  - `confirmedCarrierOf`: Normalized array of proven carrier trait names (automatically supersedes duplicates in Suspected or Cleared when promoted).
  - `clearedCarrierOf`: Array of structured non-carrier clearances `{ trait: string; proofType: 'test_bred' | 'dna_tested' | 'homozygous_locus' | 'breeder_verified'; testBreedingCount?: number; note?: string; recordedAt?: string }`.
  - Backward-compatible JSON serialization & SQLite `ALTER TABLE` column migrations so existing backups and herds upgrade seamlessly with zero data loss.
- **Species Genetics Specification**:
  - Per-species locus definitions (`autosomal`, `x_linked`, `incomplete_dominant`, `codominant`), ordered dominance hierarchies, conservative variety presets with optional `optionalConfirmations` prompts (e.g., confirming `ww` on Red), lethal/health genotype rules, and `null` fallback for user-created species.
